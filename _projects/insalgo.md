**INSAlgo** is the student association on algorithms and programming at INSA Lyon. I was its president from 2024 to 2025, and I built its current website with Jekyll.

The association runs classes open to all students, code contests, the yearly Coding Battle, install parties, and short talks on everything from dynamic programming to Rust and free software. The story of building the site is in the [blog post]({{ site.baseurl }}{% post_url 2024-08-11-new-insalgo-website %}).

**Nimbus Mk I** is a student mini-rocket I designed and launched with CLES-FACIL, the aerospace club at INSA Lyon.

It was my first real hands-on engineering project outside of software: I learned soldering, PCB design, and embedded programming along the way, and got to see the thing actually fly.

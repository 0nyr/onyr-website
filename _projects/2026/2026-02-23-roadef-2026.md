**ROADEF 2026**, the 27th annual congress of the Société Française de Recherche Opérationnelle et d'Aide à la Décision, took place from February 23 to 26, 2026 at the Université de Tours.

I presented *Randomized Constructive Heuristics for the VRPTW*: a study of the Nearest Neighbor, Best Insertion, and Regret-k constructive heuristics in a multi-start greedy randomized framework, including a novel randomization strategy for Regret-k that distinguishes the customer selection (who) from the insertion position (where). The heuristics are implemented in C++ bound to Python and evaluated on the Solomon and Gehring & Homberger benchmarks following DIMACS 2021 conventions. The extended abstract is available [on HAL](https://hal.science/hal-05646968) ([PDF](https://hal.science/hal-05646968v1/file/main.pdf)).

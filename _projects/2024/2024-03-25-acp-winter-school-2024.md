The **ACP Winter School 2024** of the Association for Constraint Programming took place from March 25 to 29, 2024 in Aussois, in the French Alps.

It was a very interesting week of presentations and lab sessions around constraint programming, a few months into my PhD, and a great occasion to meet people from the French CP and OR community.

The **OeGOR Summer School 2024** for PhD candidates and post-docs took place from July 22 to 26, 2024 at the University for Continuing Education in Krems an der Donau, Austria.

The week was split into two courses: an introduction to column-generation theory and applications by [Timo Gschwind](https://www.linkedin.com/in/timo-gschwind-672288227/), and combinatorial optimization augmented machine learning for contextual multi-stage problems by [Maximilian Schiffer](https://www.linkedin.com/in/maximilian-schiffer-319323101/). My code from the [Julia reinforcement-learning](https://github.com/0nyr/oegor_coaml_2024) as well as the [column-generation](https://github.com/0nyr/oegor_column_generation_2024) lab sessions are on GitHub.

I travelled back to Lyon by train, by choice, using an Interrail ticket.

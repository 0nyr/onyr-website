**onyr.net** is this very website: my portfolio, blog, and home base on the web, first launched in August 2024 and reworked since.

It is a static Jekyll site with a custom theme built around the Kromaryl rainbow wheel, self-hosted fonts, a light and dark mode, and no third-party requests.

**IFIP SEC 2024**, the 39th IFIP International Conference on ICT Systems Security and Privacy Protection, took place from June 12 to 14, 2024 at Edinburgh Napier University's Craiglockhart campus, in Scotland.

I presented our paper *Large Scale Heap Dump Embedding for Machine Learning: Predicting OpenSSH Key Locations*, the publication that came out of my [Mem2Graph]({{ '/projects/mem2graph/' | relative_url }}) master project at Universität Passau. See <a href="{{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}">Publications</a> for the paper.

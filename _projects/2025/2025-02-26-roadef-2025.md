**ROADEF 2025**, the 26th annual congress of the Société Française de Recherche Opérationnelle et d'Aide à la Décision, took place from February 26 to 28, 2025 on the campus of the École nationale des ponts et chaussées in Champs-sur-Marne, near Paris.

It was my first ROADEF congress, and I gave a talk there on my PhD work on the vehicle routing problem with time windows. The extended abstract, *Branch & Price & Cut for the Time-Dependent Vehicle Routing Problem with Time Windows (TDVRPTW)*, is available [on HAL](https://hal.science/hal-05646977) ([PDF](https://hal.science/hal-05646977v1/file/main.pdf)).

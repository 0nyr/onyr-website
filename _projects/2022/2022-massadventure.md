**Massadventure** is a game prototype on the MASSA blockchain my team built for the MASSA x Kryptosphere x Ledger hackathon. 

We proposed a rogue-like game inspired from NetHack and Rogue, using the autonomous smart contract and fully on-chain front using MASSA. A prototype smart-contract and front-end was successfully tested and deployed on MASSA Labnet at massa://massadventure .

With this project, we won the top 2 prize of the hackathon.

It dates from my Kryptosphere years at INSA Lyon, where I was a founding member, treasurer, and technical pole manager of the local branch from 2021 to 2022. My MASSA experiments from that period, including a small mnemonic-based private-key generator, are on GitHub.

Team: [Alexis Lemaire](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alexislemairevolpe/?locale=en), [Antoine Galas](https://www.linkedin.com/in/antoine-galas/), [Martin Alquier](https://www.linkedin.com/in/martin-alquier/?locale=en) with additional help from [Alexis Mailley](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alexis-mailley/?locale=en) and MassaLab team.

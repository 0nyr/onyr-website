**FarFarAway Swap** is a decentralized exchange (DEX) prototype my team built for the Encode Club x NEAR hackathon, during my Kryptosphere years at INSA Lyon.

The idea was to optimize liquidity by combining a classic automated market maker based on liquidity pools with an order book, in a single swap algorithm on the NEAR blockchain. The project made it to the hackathon finale and reached the top 4-7/130 projects, winning a $1500 prize for the whole team ([Nikita Terekhov](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nikita-terekhov/), [Anicet Nougaret](https://www.linkedin.com/in/anicet-nougaret-b7846b174/), [Kirill Makarov](https://www.linkedin.com/in/luaroncrew/), [Gustavo Bertoldi](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gbertoldi/)).

There was discussions to continue the project after the hackathon [into a dedicated startup](https://www.linkedin.com/company/far-swap/about/).

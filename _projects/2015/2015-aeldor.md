**AELDOR** is a fictional universe I have been building for years, set in a far future. It comes with its own worldbuilding and a constructed language, *anæwyñ*.

It is a long-running creative project rather than a shipped product, but it is the root of the *Onyr* pen name and of the Kromaryl symbol you see around this site (from the anæwyñ words *kroma* for color and *ryl* for circle).

# Publications and research output


I am a PhD student in Computer Science and Operations Research; my work centers on time-dependent vehicle routing and combinatorial optimization, within the ANR-MAMUT project (grant [ANR-22-CE22-0016](https://anr.fr/Projet-ANR-22-CE22-0016)).

[HAL: hal-05646952](https://hal.science/hal-05646952) · [PDF](https://hal.science/hal-05646952v1/file/main.pdf)
